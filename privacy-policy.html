Privacy Policy

Chess Canonical · Last updated 19 September 2026

Chess Canonical does not ask you for a name, an email address, or an account. Your lessons, puzzle rating and progress are stored only on your own device. The one thing that leaves your device is advertising data collected by Google AdMob, which pays for the app being free.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using Android's own storage:

  • Lessons and puzzles you have completed
  • Your puzzle rating and activity history
  • Achievements you have earned
  • Your settings, such as board colours, sound and playback speed

This never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to us, not backed up to any server of ours, and not shared with anyone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects information from your device, including your Android advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and data about how you interact with ads.

Google uses this to serve ads and measure how they perform. Their handling of it is covered by their own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can control this. On your device, open Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalised ads entirely. The app keeps working either way.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change that choice at any time from the app's own Settings screen.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or the moves you play. The app has no user accounts and no analytics.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Permissions

The app requests internet access. This is used only to load ads. Every lesson, every puzzle and the chess engine itself run entirely on your device and work with no connection at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Changes affecting how your information is used will also be noted in the app's release notes.
